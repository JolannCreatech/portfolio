The installation

TIN MAN’s heart looks at the barrier between a body and a machine. The visitor lends their heart to the Tin Man: their physiological signals are used to make a mechanical heart beat, so that a living rhythm passes into the object.

Borrowed signals

A heart on loan

The installation reads the visitor’s physiological signals and turns them into the behaviour of a mechanical heart.
